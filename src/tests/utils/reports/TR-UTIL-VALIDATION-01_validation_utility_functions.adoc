== Description
All assertions in `src/tests/utils/validation.test.ts` passed:

. `isValidEmail` accepts well-formed addresses and trims surrounding whitespace.
. `isValidEmail` rejects empty strings, whitespace-only inputs, addresses missing `@`, missing TLDs, double `@`, embedded spaces, and addresses longer than 254 characters.
. `isValidPassword` accepts passwords meeting the policy (>= 8 chars, lowercase, uppercase, digit).
. `isValidPassword` rejects passwords that are too short or missing any required character class; the 8-character minimum is treated as an inclusive boundary.
. `formatPhoneNumber` strips spaces, dashes, parentheses, dots, and letters while preserving a single leading `+` for international numbers.
. `formatPhoneNumber` returns an empty string for empty, whitespace-only, non-string, or digit-less inputs.
